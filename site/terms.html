What the receipt means

A receipt records what its signing key states. It cannot independently prove that a remote service completed an operation.

Your responsibility

Protect signing keys. Choose retention and redaction rules that match your organization.

Use a durable store for restart recovery. Add an external witness when independent evidence is required.
