Privacy

Privacy by construction.

This documentation site has no analytics, cookies, account system, or third-party scripts. The interactive demo runs entirely in your browser and keeps its sample state in memory until the tab closes.

Library data

The npm library makes no network requests and collects no telemetry. It hashes arguments, authorities, results, and failures. Optional redacted views are supplied by you and are included in an export only when requested. Your signer and receipt store remain under your control.
